This module adds a bank account field to Sales Teams and uses it as the Recipient Bank
on account moves when a team is assigned.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

- Go to Sales > Configuration > Sales Teams.
- Select a team and set the Bank Account field. You can only choose a bank account
  linked to the company's partner (i.e., one of the company’s own bank accounts).
