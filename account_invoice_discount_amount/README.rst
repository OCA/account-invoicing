This module shows the early payment discount totals in the invoice/bill
list, instead of requiring users to open each invoice to see them.

.. IMPORTANT::
   This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning.
   Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production.
   `More details on development status <https://odoo-community.org/page/development-status>`_

Usage
=====

1. Go to Accounting > Suppliers > Bills (or Customers > Invoices).
2. When an Early Payment Discount applies, the "Discount Amount" and
   "Discount Amount (Company Currency)" columns show the total to pay to
   benefit from it. They are hidden when there is no discount to apply.
