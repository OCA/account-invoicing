To use this module, you need to:

1.  Go to *Accounting \> Accounting \> Journals \> Extra Product Moves*.
2.  Create a new Account Product Move record and give it a name.
3.  Create Product Move Lines.
4.  Update one or more product to link them to the Extra Product Move.
5.  Create a new out invoice with lines referring to these products.
6.  Post the out invoice.
7.  If any of the products in invoice lines has an Extra Product Move
    record set-up, a new automatic journal entry will be added per line
    that refers to extra moves.

It is possible to apply an Account Product Move record only on invoices
/ moves that meet certain criteria. To do this, filter your moves, and
store the filter with sharing set. Then select this filter on the
Account Product Move record.
