1.  Go to *Purchases \> Purchase Orders \> Create*.
2.  Choose a supplier and add a stockable product.
3.  Confirm the purchase order.
4.  Go to *Receptions \> Validate \> Apply*.
5.  Return to the purchase order.
6.  Press the button *Create Bill* to create the vendor bill.
7.  Set accounting date with other month date
8.  Press the button *Confirm* to view warning message.


Comparison by date (by day)
----------------------------------
1. Go to *Settings \> Invoicing \> Customer Invoices*
2. Check option *Accounting Date Comparison*
3. When making comparisons between the accounting date and picking,
   the day will be taken into account.
