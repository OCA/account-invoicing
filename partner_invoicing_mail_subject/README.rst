This module extends the functionality of Invoice mailing to allow
partners to have individual invoice email subjects. It ensures that the
partner-specific subject is used when sending invoices, both for single
and batch invoice emails.

-  Adds a new field invoice_email_subject on partners (commercial
   partners) to define a custom subject line for invoice emails.
-  Automatically uses the partner-specific subject when sending invoices
   via email.
-  If no individual subject is set, the standard email template subject
   is used.
-  Works for both single invoice and batch invoice sending.

Usage
=====

To use this module, you need to:

1. Go to Contacts and open a Partner (Commercial Partner) form.
2. In the Invoicing tab, under the Customer Invoices section set
   invoice_sending_method to 'email'
3. Enter a custom subject line for invoice emails in the field "Invoice
   Email Subject". You can use placeholders such as {invoice_number},
   {partner_name}, and {invoice_date}

When sending invoices via email (single or batch), the partner-specific
subject will be automatically used. If no custom subject is set for a
partner, the standard email template subject will be used.
