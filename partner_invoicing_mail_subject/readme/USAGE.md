To use this module, you need to:

1. Go to Contacts and open a Partner (Commercial Partner) form.
2. In the Invoicing tab, under the Customer Invoices section set invoice_sending_method to 'email'
3. Enter a custom subject line for invoice emails in the field "Invoice Email Subject". You can use placeholders
such as {invoice_number}, {partner_name}, and {invoice_date}

When sending invoices via email (single or batch), the partner-specific subject will be automatically used.
If no custom subject is set for a partner, the standard email template subject will be used.
