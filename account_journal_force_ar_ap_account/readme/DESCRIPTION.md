This module allows you to configure an accounts receivable or accounts 
payable account on sales and purchase journals, respectively. 
When an account is configured on the journal, invoices posted 
using that journal will use the account defined on the journal 
instead of the accounts receivable or accounts payable account 
assigned to the customer or vendor, as applicable.

The same field is used in the journal for both types of accounts. 
The accounts available for selection are determined by a dynamic 
domain that filters the accounts based on the journal type.
