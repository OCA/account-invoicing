In some accounting setups, multiple taxes may share the same rate (e.g. 21%) but differ
in their reporting usage depending on the nature of the expense.

For example:
- Accounts starting with 60 → goods
- Accounts starting with 61 → services
- Accounts starting with 2 → investments

Standard tax selection based solely on the rate is therefore not sufficient
to identify the correct tax.

This module introduces a simple and configurable mechanism to restrict tax selection
based on one or more account code prefixes.
