1.  Go to *Invoicing \> Customers \> Invoices* or *Invoicing \> Vendors
    \> Bills*.
2.  Mark at least one check on the left part of one draft invoice in the
    list view.
3.  Click on *Action \> Post entries*.
4.  On the dialog popup that appears, click on "Enqueue Validation".
5.  Once enqueued, and having the "Job Queue Manager" permission, you
    can go to the invoice, and see the tab "Validation Jobs". A list
    with all the jobs related to that invoice can be found there.
