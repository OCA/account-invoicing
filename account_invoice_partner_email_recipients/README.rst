This module extends Odoo's invoice sending functionality to allow
configuring multiple email recipients for customer invoices and credit
notes.

By default in standard Odoo, invoices sent by email are only addressed
to the single primary commercial partner unless extra recipients are
manually selected every time the send wizard is opened. During batch
sending or automated background sending, extra recipients cannot be
specified.

With this module:

- Dedicated invoice email recipients can be configured per customer on
  the partner form.
- Invoices automatically inherit these recipients, with the option for
  accountants to add or remove recipients per invoice while in draft.
- When sending invoices (individually via the wizard, in batch, or via
  automated crons), all configured recipients are automatically included
  in the email distribution.

Usage
=====

To use this module:

1. Go to **Contacts** or **Invoicing > Customers > Customers** and
   select a customer.
2. In the **Invoicing** tab under **Customer Invoices**, locate the
   **Invoice Recipients** field.
3. Select the additional partner contacts (e.g., Accounts Payable,
   billing managers) who should receive invoices. Ensure these contacts
   have valid email addresses.
4. When creating a customer invoice for this partner, the **Invoice
   Recipients** field under **Other Info** is automatically populated.
   You can add or remove recipients on the draft invoice if needed.
5. Send the invoice:

   - **Single send**: Click **Send & Print**. The "To" field in the
     sending wizard is automatically pre-filled with the customer and
     all configured recipients.
   - **Batch send**: Select multiple invoices in the list view and click
     **Send & Print**. Each invoice will be emailed to its primary
     customer plus all configured invoice recipients.
   - **Automated sending**: If automated sending is enabled (e.g., via
     ``account_invoice_auto_send_by_email``), the background job will
     dispatch the invoice to all configured recipients.
