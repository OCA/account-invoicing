This module lets accounting users assign a structured overdue reason to
customer invoices whose due date has passed.

The reason can then be used from the invoice list to identify, filter,
and group overdue invoices during payment follow-up.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Use Cases / Context
===================

Customer payment follow-up often needs a clear reason why an invoice
remains unpaid: dispute, missing purchase order, customer promise to
pay, internal validation, or another operational cause. Without a
structured reason, this information is usually spread across notes and
activities, making overdue invoice reporting harder to review.

This module adds a configurable overdue reason on customer invoices once
their due date has passed. Accounting teams can use this reason to
classify overdue invoices and group or filter invoice lists during
collection follow-up.

Configuration
=============

To configure overdue reasons:

- Go to *Invoicing / Accounting > Configuration > Overdue Reasons*

Usage
=====

- Open a customer invoice whose due date is in the past
- Set the *Overdue Reason* field on the invoice
