Customer payment follow-up often needs a clear reason why an invoice remains
unpaid: dispute, missing purchase order, customer promise to pay, internal
validation, or another operational cause.
Without a structured reason, this information is usually spread across notes
and activities, making overdue invoice reporting harder to review.

This module adds a configurable overdue reason on customer invoices once
their due date has passed. Accounting teams can use this reason to
classify overdue invoices and group or filter invoice lists during collection
follow-up.
